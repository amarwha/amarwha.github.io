MARRASSI AL BAHRAIN MALL

Marrassi Al Bahrain is a prestigious waterfront development on the northern coast of Bahrain, master-planned as an integrated urban community. The mall component serves as the commercial anchor of the wider masterplan, offering a curated retail and leisure destination within a distinctive coastal setting. BIM coordination services were delivered across architectural, structural, and MEP disciplines, supporting the design and construction of this complex multi-storey facility.
