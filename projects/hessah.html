HESSAH AL MUBARAK DISTRICT

Hessah Al Mubarak District is a large-scale mixed-use urban development located in Kuwait City. The project encompasses residential towers, retail podiums, and civic amenities, forming a cohesive district typology within the existing urban fabric. As BIM Consultant, the scope included the development and coordination of federated building information models across architectural, structural, and MEP disciplines, ensuring design intent was accurately translated into construction documentation.
