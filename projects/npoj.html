NEW PALACE OF JUSTICE KUWAIT

The New Palace of Justice Kuwait is a landmark civic institution commissioned to consolidate and modernise Kuwait's judicial infrastructure under a single architectural statement. The design reflects the gravity and permanence of the institution, combining monumental civic expression with functional planning. BIM consultancy services encompassed full model development, multi-disciplinary coordination, clash resolution, and the production of government-standard construction documentation.
