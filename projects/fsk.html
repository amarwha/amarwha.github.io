FUTURE SCHOOLS OF KUWAIT

The Future Schools of Kuwait is a government programme to design and deliver a new generation of school buildings across the country. The initiative required the development of standardised prototype designs adaptable across varied sites and conditions. BIM played a central role throughout, enabling consistent documentation, rapid design iteration, and coordinated delivery across multiple school buildings simultaneously.
