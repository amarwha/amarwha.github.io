HILTON GARDEN INN

The Hilton Garden Inn project involved the BIM-enabled design and documentation of a branded mid-scale hospitality development. The scope required close alignment with Hilton's global brand standards and technical specifications, delivering a fully coordinated building information model encompassing architectural, structural, and MEP systems. The project represents an early application of BIM in the hospitality sector within the region.
